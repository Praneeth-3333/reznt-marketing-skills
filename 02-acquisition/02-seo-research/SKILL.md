# SEO & Organic Search Research Skill

## Purpose
Find search demand, intent, content gaps, and technical/on-page opportunities that can bring relevant businesses to Reznt.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target audience, geography, language.
- Site pages or competitor domains.
- Goal: non-branded traffic, demos, signups, or topical authority.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Build seed topics around AI revenue operators, WhatsApp AI agents, lead qualification, missed-call recovery, follow-up automation, Instagram enquiry handling, and vertical-specific workflows.
2. Expand keyword candidates using current keyword tools/search results where available. Do not invent search volume or difficulty.
3. Classify intent: informational, comparison, solution-aware, transactional, branded, or local.
4. Map keywords to one primary page each; avoid creating near-duplicate pages that compete with one another.
5. Analyze search results and competitor pages for intent, format, gaps, freshness, and evidence requirements.
6. Audit title/meta, headings, internal links, page speed/indexing/canonical/schema where tooling supports it.
7. Prioritize by relevance, intent, evidence of demand, ranking feasibility, conversion fit, and effort.
8. Create a brief with audience, intent, outline, internal links, proof needed, CTA, and measurement.

## Required output
- Keyword map with intent and source/method.
- Content-gap analysis and prioritized page roadmap.
- Technical/on-page audit with verified issues only.
- Briefs for priority pages and suggested internal links.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not fabricate keyword volumes, rankings, backlinks, or technical test results. Keep content useful and original; avoid doorway pages and unsupported claims.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.
