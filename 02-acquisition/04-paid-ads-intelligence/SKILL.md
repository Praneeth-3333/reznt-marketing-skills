# Paid Ads & Creative Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Develop evidence-led paid acquisition hypotheses for Reznt using competitor ads, search intent, and creative testing.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment/geography.
- Platform(s), budget range, and conversion goal.
- Existing campaign results if available.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Review current ad policies and platform requirements relevant to the campaign.
2. Inspect available public ad libraries and search ads; record creative, copy, CTA, landing page, apparent audience, and dates where available.
3. Group creative angles: missed revenue, speed to first reply, follow-up consistency, unified context, industry-specific workflows, and human-controlled automation.
4. Match each angle to an ICP and funnel stage.
5. Design a test matrix changing one major variable at a time where feasible.
6. Define conversion tracking, attribution, lead-quality feedback, budget caps, and stop/scale rules.
7. Recommend landing-page alignment and lead follow-up process.

## Required output
- Competitor ad gallery/index with URLs and dates.
- Creative angle matrix and ad brief concepts.
- Audience and landing-page hypotheses.
- Experiment plan and measurement checklist.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not copy competitor creative. Do not promise results, target sensitive attributes improperly, or invent competitor spend/performance.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.
