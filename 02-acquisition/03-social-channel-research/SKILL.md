# Social Channel & Community Research Skill

## Purpose
Research how Reznt can earn attention and qualified demand on Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, and relevant communities.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target ICP and channel(s).
- Geography/language.
- Goal and content capacity.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define the audience and buying problem for each channel.
2. Review relevant competitor accounts, creators, public posts, comments, formats, and community discussions.
3. Record post date, format, visible engagement, hook, audience response, and URL. Do not assume engagement equals pipeline.
4. Identify repeatable themes: missed enquiries, response delays, lead qualification, follow-up, customer memory, human approval, use-case demos, and industry playbooks.
5. Separate channel-native formats and audience expectations; do not simply cross-post identical content everywhere.
6. Propose content experiments with a hook, format, CTA, target segment, production effort, and success metric.
7. Build a short calendar and learning loop.

## Required output
- Channel-specific audience insights.
- Competitor/content pattern table with source links.
- Content pillars, post concepts, hooks, and test calendar.
- Metrics and review plan.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not buy followers, fabricate engagement, or present public engagement as conversion evidence. Respect platform terms and community rules.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.
