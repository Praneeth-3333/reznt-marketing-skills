# Channel Opportunity Research Skill

## Purpose
Compare potential acquisition channels for a defined Reznt segment and recommend the best next test.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target ICP/vertical and geography.
- Goal: leads, demos, signups, or awareness.
- Available budget, team capacity, and time horizon if known.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Consider SEO, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Meta Ads, Google Ads, outbound, partnerships, events, communities, referrals, and PR.
2. For each channel, research audience presence, intent, competitor activity, creative/content norms, cost proxies, lead quality signals, and implementation requirements.
3. Estimate effort and time-to-signal; label any estimates as hypotheses unless supported by actual data.
4. Score channel fit, buyer intent, reachability, evidence strength, speed to test, and measurement feasibility.
5. Recommend a small channel portfolio: one primary test, one secondary test, and one longer-term asset.
6. Define test design, target cohort, budget/effort cap, success metric, stop/continue rule, and learning questions.
7. Feed results into analytics once real data is available.

## Required output
- Channel comparison and scoring.
- Recommended tests with rationale.
- Execution requirements and measurement plan.
- Assumptions, risks, and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not promise CAC or ROAS without campaign data. Distinguish platform benchmarks from Reznt's actual performance.
