# Email & Lifecycle Channel Research Skill

## Purpose
Research email as a channel for B2B demand generation, lead nurture, education, and customer lifecycle communication.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Audience/segment and geography.
- Goal and available sending platform.
- Consent, list source, deliverability data, and campaign constraints.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define the lifecycle job and audience.
2. Research audience needs, inbox norms, relevant competitor newsletters, and content gaps.
3. Review applicable email marketing and privacy requirements for the target geography.
4. Recommend segmentation, value proposition, content cadence, and clear opt-out.
5. Draft subject-line and message hypotheses tailored to the audience.
6. Define deliverability safeguards and metrics such as delivery, click, reply, qualified conversion, unsubscribe, and complaint rates.
7. Design a small test and a review process.

## Required output
- Channel opportunity brief.
- Segment and journey plan.
- Email concepts and test matrix.
- Compliance/deliverability checklist and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not recommend purchased lists or deceptive subject lines. Verify consent and legal requirements before sending.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.
