# Outbound & Prospecting Research Skill

## Purpose
Identify suitable businesses and develop respectful, personalized outreach hypotheses for Reznt.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- ICP/vertical and geography.
- Approved source types and outreach channel.
- Outreach goal and applicable consent/compliance constraints.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Translate ICP into observable business-fit signals such as enquiry-heavy operations, multiple locations, appointment/order workflows, or active messaging channels.
2. Find organizations from permitted public business sources; collect only business-relevant data needed for the task.
3. Verify each prospect's website and relevance. Do not guess contact details or invent personalization.
4. Create a concise hypothesis about a workflow Reznt may help with, grounded in visible evidence.
5. Draft a short, transparent message that identifies the sender, gives a relevant reason for contact, avoids deceptive familiarity, and offers an easy opt-out where appropriate.
6. Create a follow-up sequence that is limited, useful, and compliant with channel rules.
7. Define qualification criteria and track positive replies, opt-outs, meetings, and qualified pipeline.

## Required output
- Prospect criteria and qualified prospect list with source URLs.
- Personalized outreach drafts and follow-up cadence.
- Qualification rubric and tracking fields.
- Compliance checklist and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not scrape private personal data, bypass platform restrictions, or send unsolicited messages without confirming lawful basis, consent, and channel policy. Do not automatically send outreach unless the user explicitly authorizes it and tools support it.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
