# Partnerships & Distribution Research Skill

## Purpose
Find credible partners who can introduce Reznt to businesses with conversation-heavy workflows.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment/geography.
- Partner types of interest: agencies, consultants, software providers, creators, associations, or resellers.
- Partnership goal and commercial constraints.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define partner value exchange and ideal partner profile.
2. Discover potential partners through official directories, integration ecosystems, industry associations, agencies, consultants, and public creator profiles.
3. Verify relevance, audience, services, geography, credibility, and contact route from public sources.
4. Identify co-marketing, referral, integration, reseller, webinar, or educational opportunities.
5. Score strategic fit, audience overlap, trust, activation effort, potential reach, and conflict risk.
6. Draft a tailored partnership hypothesis and first-contact message.
7. Recommend a small pilot with explicit success criteria and ownership.

## Required output
- Partner longlist and scored shortlist with source links.
- Partner value proposition and proposed collaboration.
- Outreach draft and pilot plan.
- Risks, dependencies, and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not imply a partnership exists until confirmed. Verify integration claims and avoid using partner logos without permission.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.
