# Content & Creative Research Skill

## Purpose
Turn verified customer, market, and product insights into a repeatable content pipeline for Reznt.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target ICP, channel, content goal, and timeframe.
- Existing content or brand constraints.
- Evidence/research records to use, if available.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Gather verified findings from VoC, ICP, vertical, SEO, competitor, and social research.
2. Choose content pillars: problem awareness, workflow education, use-case demonstrations, trust/governance, industry playbooks, product explainers, and customer proof.
3. Identify audience awareness stage and the question each asset answers.
4. Generate concepts with a specific hook, narrative, format, proof requirement, CTA, and repurposing path.
5. Tailor concepts to platform behavior: Reels, carousels, LinkedIn posts, YouTube demos, blogs, newsletters, or webinars.
6. Mark any claim requiring product, legal, or customer approval.
7. Prioritize by audience relevance, proof strength, distinctiveness, production effort, and commercial intent.

## Required output
- Content pillars and audience/message map.
- Ranked ideas with hooks, formats, CTA, proof needed, and source evidence.
- A calendar or production brief.
- Repurposing plan and success metrics.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not fabricate customer stories, testimonials, product screenshots, metrics, or trend claims. Avoid generic AI hype and repetitive slogans.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
