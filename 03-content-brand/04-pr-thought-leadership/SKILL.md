# PR & Thought Leadership Research Skill

## Purpose
Find media, speaking, podcast, newsletter, and expert-content opportunities that match Reznt's market and expertise.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target audience/geography.
- Founder/spokesperson expertise and approved topics.
- Goal: awareness, authority, partner reach, or demand.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define credible topics Reznt can contribute to: conversational commerce, AI operations, lead follow-up, customer experience, and human-governed automation.
2. Identify relevant publications, podcasts, newsletters, events, journalists, and community leaders using public information.
3. Verify current relevance, audience, recent coverage, submission route, and editorial fit.
4. Develop non-promotional angles supported by evidence, original insight, or useful data.
5. Prepare a pitch brief with hook, timeliness, spokesperson, proof, and audience benefit.
6. Prioritize by fit, credibility, reach, effort, and likelihood of meaningful engagement.
7. Track outreach and outcomes.

## Required output
- Media/creator/event shortlist with source URLs.
- Tailored story angles and pitch drafts.
- Spokesperson proof points and evidence needs.
- Outreach tracker fields and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not claim media relationships, awards, or endorsements that do not exist. Respect journalists' preferences and do not mass-spam.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
