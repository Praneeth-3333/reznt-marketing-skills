# Conversion Copywriting & Messaging Skill

## Purpose
Write clear, audience-specific Reznt messaging grounded in the audience's problem and verified product capabilities.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Asset type: landing page, ad, email, social post, outbound, demo script, or case study.
- Target audience and funnel stage.
- Desired action, tone, length, and evidence.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define audience, pain, desired outcome, current alternative, objection, and next action.
2. Pull evidence from the brand brief, VoC, ICP, and product documentation.
3. Choose one main message and supporting proof points.
4. Draft variants using distinct angles, not superficial synonym changes.
5. Ensure every product statement is verifiable and the CTA matches audience readiness.
6. Annotate claims that require proof, approval, or legal review.
7. Recommend a test plan and success metric.

## Required output
- Primary copy draft and 2–4 meaningfully different variants when useful.
- Message rationale and audience fit.
- Proof/approval checklist.
- Suggested test and measurement plan.

## Quality and safety checks
Never invent testimonials, customer numbers, ROI, guarantees, integrations, or product behavior. Do not imply the AI always acts without human review.
