# Brand & Reputation Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Monitor how Reznt and the category are described publicly and identify trust-building opportunities.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Brand/category and geography.
- Sources/channels to monitor.
- Reputation question or campaign context.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Review Reznt's public messaging and approved brand guidelines.
2. Find public mentions, reviews, social discussion, press coverage, and competitor/category narratives.
3. Categorize themes: clarity, trust, reliability, privacy, control, usefulness, price, setup effort, and perceived risk.
4. Separate direct evidence about Reznt from general category sentiment.
5. Identify gaps in proof, FAQs, security explanations, onboarding communication, and differentiation.
6. Recommend response principles and content that addresses genuine concerns.
7. Escalate security, privacy, legal, or serious customer issues to the responsible team.

## Required output
- Brand narrative summary and public sentiment themes.
- Evidence table with URLs and dates.
- Trust gaps and recommended actions.
- Escalation list and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not fabricate sentiment scores from small samples. Do not reveal private customer details or respond publicly without authorization.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
