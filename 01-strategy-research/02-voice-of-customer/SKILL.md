# Voice of Customer (VoC) Research Skill

## Purpose
Discover the words customers use, the problems they experience, and what makes them adopt or reject tools like Reznt.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment, geography, and research question.
- Public review/interview URLs or authorized internal feedback.
- Any existing approved customer quotes or case-study evidence.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define the question: pain, trigger, desired outcome, objections, alternatives, trust, price, or buying process.
2. Gather appropriate sources: public reviews, forums, social posts, interviews, support themes, sales call notes, and surveys. Use authorized data only.
3. Capture verbatim quotes only with a source, context, date, and permission/usage check. Otherwise paraphrase and label as a summary.
4. Code findings into themes: response delays, lead leakage, repetitive questions, follow-up workload, customer context, channel fragmentation, governance/trust, integrations, cost, and change management.
5. Note the segment and situation for each theme; do not generalize a single comment to the whole market.
6. Compare positive, negative, and neutral signals. Identify unmet needs and objections to AI automation.
7. Translate themes into messaging hypotheses, product questions, and interview prompts.

## Required output
- Top customer pain themes ranked by frequency in the sample and severity.
- Exact sourced quotes where appropriate, with URLs and dates.
- Desired outcomes, buying triggers, objections, alternatives, and trust concerns.
- Messaging opportunities and questions requiring validation.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Never fabricate quotes or imply a public commenter is a Reznt customer. Distinguish frequency in a sample from prevalence in the market. Respect privacy and terms of source platforms.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
