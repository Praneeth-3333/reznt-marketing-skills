# Industry / Vertical Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Understand one industry’s customer conversation workflows and identify specific Reznt use cases and go-to-market messages.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Industry and subsegment.
- Geography/language.
- Business size or operating model, if known.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Map the industry's enquiry-to-revenue journey: first contact, qualification, quote, appointment/order, follow-up, fulfillment, review, repeat purchase.
2. Identify channels used and common question types through credible public evidence and interviews.
3. Research operational constraints, seasonal patterns, buying roles, compliance concerns, and existing software.
4. Match each workflow to Reznt features only where current product evidence supports the fit.
5. Identify routine actions vs. actions requiring approval or human judgment.
6. Propose measurable pilot outcomes such as first-response time, qualified lead rate, booking rate, follow-up completion, recovered enquiries, or repeat purchase.
7. Recommend vertical-specific landing-page themes, demo scenarios, objections, and discovery questions.

## Required output
- Vertical brief and customer journey.
- Top workflow opportunities, sorted by fit and urgency.
- Buyer roles, objections, buying triggers, and proof needed.
- Product-fit map: supported / verify / not established.
- Pilot measurement plan and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not provide medical, legal, financial, or other regulated-industry advice. Flag sector-specific privacy and compliance requirements for expert review.
