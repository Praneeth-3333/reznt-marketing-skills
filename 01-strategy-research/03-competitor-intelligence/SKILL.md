# Competitor Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Map alternatives to Reznt and find defensible positioning opportunities using current, verifiable evidence.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment/geography.
- Competitor names or permission to discover them.
- Comparison focus: product, price, channel, positioning, ads, SEO, or customer sentiment.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define competitive sets: direct WhatsApp/Instagram AI agents, messaging automation platforms, CRM/workflow tools, customer-support AI, human/manual alternatives, and vertical-specific tools.
2. Find competitors through search, directories, official pages, app marketplaces, review sites, and public ad libraries.
3. For each competitor, capture official positioning, audience, use cases, verified channels, features, pricing model, onboarding, integrations, trust controls, and proof.
4. Review their landing pages, calls to action, content, and public customer feedback. Record source URLs and dates.
5. Compare with Reznt only using verified Reznt capabilities from the brand brief and current official pages.
6. Identify category conventions, underserved needs, possible differentiation, and messaging gaps.
7. Create a battlecard for the most relevant competitors and a watchlist for material changes.

## Required output
- Competitive landscape map by category.
- Comparison matrix with evidence links and “unknown/not stated” where necessary.
- Top 3–5 competitor battlecards.
- Positioning opportunities and risks for Reznt.
- Recommended tests and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not treat competitor marketing claims as verified performance. Never infer a feature from a logo or vague wording. Check pricing and feature availability on official pages. Avoid defamatory language.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
