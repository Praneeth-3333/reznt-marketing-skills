# ICP & Niche Discovery Skill

## Purpose
Find and prioritize customer segments most likely to benefit from Reznt's AI Revenue Operator, using evidence rather than assumptions.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target geography and language.
- Candidate industries or permission to discover them.
- Current product constraints, ideal deal size, and sales motion if known.
- Any existing customer, pipeline, or win/loss data the user is authorized to share.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Translate Reznt's public positioning into testable needs: high inbound conversation volume, repeated questions, missed calls, delayed follow-up, lead qualification, booking/order support, repeat-customer recovery, and limited staff capacity.
2. Build a longlist of verticals and use cases. Include Reznt website segments such as real estate, clinics/wellness, dealerships, education/coaching, travel, retail/D2C, home services, and restaurants; do not assume they are equally attractive.
3. Research each segment using primary sources, industry communities, job descriptions, public reviews, business websites, and relevant reports.
4. Score segments 1–5 on pain severity, frequency of conversations, potential revenue value, fit with Reznt's current verified capabilities, ability to reach buyers, urgency, and evidence strength.
5. Identify buying roles, triggers, current workaround, likely objections, and measurable outcomes.
6. Separate verified facts from hypotheses. Recommend a small validation sprint, such as customer interviews or a tightly scoped outbound test.
7. Rank the top 3–5 segments and explain why the others are lower priority.

## Required output
- Executive recommendation and top segment ranking.
- Segment scoring table with rationale.
- ICP profile: firmographics, buying roles, conversation workflow, pains, trigger events, objections, success metrics.
- Highest-value use cases and message angles for each segment.
- Validation plan and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not invent market size or claim a segment is profitable without data. Avoid using sensitive personal data. State sample sizes and research limitations. Fit must be checked against current product capabilities.
