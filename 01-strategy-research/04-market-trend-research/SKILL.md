# Market & Trend Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Identify changes in customer behavior, technology, regulation, and category demand that may affect Reznt's growth.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Market or topic to investigate.
- Geography and time horizon.
- Decision the research should inform.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Define the trend and decision scope.
2. Search current reputable reports, primary platform announcements, official statistics, company announcements, and practitioner evidence.
3. Record publication date, geography, sample/methodology, and source limitations.
4. Separate structural trends from short-lived social buzz.
5. Assess implications for conversation-led businesses and Reznt's product, acquisition channels, positioning, or risk.
6. Rank opportunities by relevance, evidence, likely impact, and ability to test.
7. Recommend what to monitor and what would invalidate each hypothesis.

## Required output
- Trend brief with evidence and dates.
- Signal vs. noise assessment.
- Implications for Reznt.
- Ranked opportunities, threats, and watchlist.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not cite a statistic without its methodology/context. Avoid treating forecasts as outcomes. For time-sensitive topics, verify freshness before reporting.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

## Research logging
For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
