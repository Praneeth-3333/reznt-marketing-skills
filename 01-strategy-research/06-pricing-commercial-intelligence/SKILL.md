# Pricing & Commercial Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Research pricing expectations, competitor packaging, and value metrics to inform Reznt's commercial strategy.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment/geography.
- Pricing question or plan to evaluate.
- Current official Reznt pricing URL or permission to check it.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Verify current Reznt pricing, trial terms, credit model, included features, taxes, and add-ons from the official pricing page.
2. Select relevant competitors and capture public prices, billing cadence, limits, overages, setup fees, and feature gates.
3. Normalize prices carefully; note currency, tax, seat/number/conversation/credit basis, and date.
4. Research buyer value drivers and alternatives, including staff time, missed opportunities, lead volume, and operational costs. Do not assign values without evidence.
5. Model transparent scenarios with explicit assumptions; show sensitivity to volume and usage.
6. Identify packaging confusion, likely objections, and value metrics worth testing through interviews.
7. Recommend experiments, not unsubstantiated price changes.

## Required output
- Dated pricing comparison.
- Comparable unit economics where possible, with assumptions shown.
- Value-driver and objection map.
- Pricing research gaps and interview questions.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Prices change. Recheck before publication. Do not imply a competitor price is directly comparable if usage units differ. Do not invent willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.
