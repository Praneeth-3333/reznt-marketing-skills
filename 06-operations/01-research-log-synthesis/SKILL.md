# Research Log, Synthesis & Knowledge Management Skill

## Purpose
Convert separate skill outputs into a searchable, consistent, non-duplicative marketing knowledge base.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Research files/log records or access to the team's authorized knowledge system.
- Question or decision to synthesize.
- Required output format.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Read the available records and identify dates, sources, evidence labels, segments, and owners.
2. Normalize tags, naming, dates, industries, channels, and status without changing the original meaning.
3. Detect duplicate or conflicting findings; preserve provenance and show conflicts rather than silently resolving them.
4. Summarize what is known, what is uncertain, and which findings are stale.
5. Link related records across ICP, competitor, VoC, channel, content, and analytics work.
6. Produce a decision-oriented synthesis with recommendations and gaps.
7. Suggest review dates and owners for volatile findings.
8. If no persistent storage integration is available, return clean Markdown/CSV-ready records and clearly state that no external system was updated.

## Required output
- Synthesized brief with linked records.
- Duplicate/conflict/staleness report.
- Updated tags and proposed review dates.
- Decision log and follow-up research tasks.

## Quality and safety checks
Never invent a source or silently overwrite contradictory evidence. Do not claim a database was updated unless the connected system confirms it.
