# WhatsApp & Conversational Ecosystem Research Skill

## Purpose
Research the messaging-platform ecosystem, customer conversation patterns, and relevant policy requirements for Reznt's marketing and product use cases.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Topic: platform feature, API, consent, messaging rules, channel comparison, or use case.
- Geography and use case.
- Intended decision or content.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Start with official Meta/WhatsApp documentation and relevant local regulator guidance.
2. Check publication/update dates and applicability to the precise API/product and use case.
3. Separate policy requirements from recommended practice and from commercial interpretation.
4. Research conversation workflows and business use cases through credible sources and customer evidence.
5. Identify consent, opt-in, template, messaging-window, opt-out, data handling, and escalation issues where applicable; verify exact current rules rather than relying on memory.
6. Explain implications for Reznt's product claims, campaigns, and customer education.
7. Flag legal/privacy questions for qualified review.

## Required output
- Dated source-linked policy summary.
- Relevant requirements and practical checklist.
- Implications for marketing/product messaging.
- Uncertainties and expert-review items.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Not legal advice. Policies change frequently; verify at time of use. Never advise bypassing consent, platform restrictions, or enforcement.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
