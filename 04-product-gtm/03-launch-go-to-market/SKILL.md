# Launch & Go-to-Market Planning Skill

## Purpose
Build a practical launch plan for a Reznt feature, segment, campaign, or geographic expansion.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Launch scope and date/window.
- Target audience and desired outcome.
- Product readiness, assets, budget, team, and constraints.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Confirm the launch goal and audience.
2. Map readiness: product capability, support readiness, pricing, onboarding, analytics, compliance, documentation, and demo environment.
3. Define audience segments, problem narrative, positioning, proof, objections, and call to action.
4. Select channels based on evidence and available resources.
5. Build phases: research, preparation, launch, feedback, optimization.
6. Assign owners, deliverables, dependencies, and dates.
7. Define launch metrics and decision checkpoints.
8. Prepare a risk register and fallback plan.

## Required output
- Launch brief and positioning.
- Audience/channel plan and calendar.
- Asset checklist and owners.
- Measurement plan, risk register, and post-launch review template.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not announce features or dates that are not approved. Distinguish committed launch facts from tentative plans.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.
