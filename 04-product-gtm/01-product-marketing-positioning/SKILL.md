# Product Marketing & Positioning Skill

## Purpose
Translate Reznt's product capabilities into differentiated, evidence-backed value propositions for specific audiences.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Target segment and geography.
- Product feature or use case.
- Competitor/alternative context and verified product documentation.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Establish the user's job, workflow, pain, desired outcome, current alternative, and buying criteria.
2. Confirm current Reznt capabilities from official pages and internal product owners where available.
3. Map feature → workflow change → business value → metric to validate.
4. Compare the positioning with direct competitors and manual/legacy alternatives.
5. Create a positioning statement, value proposition, message pillars, proof requirements, objections, and demo narrative.
6. Identify claims that need product/legal approval.
7. Recommend a test with a specific segment and measurable result.

## Required output
- Positioning statement and category framing.
- Audience-specific value proposition and message hierarchy.
- Feature-benefit-proof map.
- Competitor differentiation and objection handling.
- Demo story and validation plan.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not claim uniqueness without a competitive review. Distinguish product capability from customer outcome and outcome hypothesis.
