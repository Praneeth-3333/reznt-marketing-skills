# Research Standards for All Reznt Skills

## Required behavior

1. Clarify the decision the research is intended to support.
2. Identify audience, industry, geography, language, channel, and timeframe.
3. Prefer primary sources: official product/pricing pages, platform documentation, original interviews, first-party analytics, public ad libraries, and original research.
4. For every material finding, record the source URL, source type, date accessed, and what it actually supports.
5. Separate **observed fact**, **source-reported claim**, **interpretation**, and **hypothesis**.
6. Verify time-sensitive claims immediately before publication.
7. Use more than one independent source for important strategic claims when practical.
8. Quote customers exactly only when the source is available and quoting is permitted. Do not invent representative quotes.
9. Do not fabricate data, source URLs, search volumes, ad spend, market size, conversion rates, customer outcomes, or competitor features.
10. If evidence is unavailable, state the gap and suggest how to test it.
11. Do not use private customer data unless access is authorized and the intended use is approved.
12. Follow applicable privacy laws, data-protection requirements, WhatsApp/Meta policies, email rules, and consent requirements.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified:** Directly supported by a reliable primary source.
- **Corroborated:** Supported by multiple independent credible sources.
- **Directional:** A useful signal from limited evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** Plausible but not yet validated.
- **Unknown:** Not enough evidence to make a responsible claim.

## Research scoring

Score each opportunity from 1–5 on:
- Relevance to Reznt's positioning
- Pain severity
- Evidence strength
- Commercial potential
- Ease/speed of testing

State the scoring rationale. Do not present subjective scores as objective facts.

## Recency guidance

- Pricing, platform rules, product availability, and policy: check at time of use.
- Competitor positioning and features: ideally check within 30 days before a major decision.
- Trends and social formats: check within 7–30 days depending on how fast the topic changes.
- Evergreen concepts: refresh when evidence or market conditions change.

## Required final response structure

1. **Executive takeaway** — 3–5 bullets.
2. **Scope** — audience, geography, period, channels, assumptions.
3. **Findings** — evidence-led, with citations/links.
4. **Interpretation** — what findings may mean for Reznt.
5. **Recommendations** — prioritized, actionable, and tied to a reason.
6. **Confidence and gaps** — what remains uncertain.
7. **Research-log records** — ready to copy into the shared log.

## Ethical guardrails

- Do not scrape or expose private personal data.
- Do not bypass access controls or platform limits.
- Do not spam, misrepresent identity, or recommend deceptive engagement.
- Do not use confidential competitor information.
- For outbound marketing, verify lawful basis/consent and channel-specific rules before sending.
- Escalate legal, privacy, medical, financial, or sensitive claims to the appropriate owner.
