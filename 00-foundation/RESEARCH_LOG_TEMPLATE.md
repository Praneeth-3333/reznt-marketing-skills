## Example (illustrative hypothesis; not a verified finding)

- Research question: Which real estate enquiry workflow might benefit from faster responses?
- Finding: Property availability and viewing requests may be suitable for routine-answer and lead-qualification workflows.
- Evidence label: Hypothesis
- Interpretation: Test whether agents receive enough repeated questions and whether delayed replies correlate with missed viewings.
- Recommended action: Interview 5–8 real estate operators and map the enquiry-to-viewing process.
- Confidence: Low until validated.
