# Marketing Metrics Glossary

Use consistent definitions across skills. Align definitions with Reznt's actual analytics and finance reporting before making business decisions.

- **Impressions:** Times content/ad was displayed; platform definitions vary.
- **Reach:** Estimated unique accounts/people exposed; platform definitions vary.
- **CTR:** Clicks ÷ impressions.
- **Landing-page conversion rate:** Defined conversion events ÷ eligible landing-page sessions/visitors. State the denominator.
- **Lead:** A person or business meeting a defined capture criterion.
- **MQL / qualified lead:** Use Reznt's agreed qualification criteria; never assume every enquiry is qualified.
- **Demo booking rate:** Booked demos ÷ stated eligible denominator.
- **Show rate:** Attended demos ÷ booked demos for the same cohort.
- **Lead-to-customer conversion:** New customers ÷ leads in a defined cohort and window.
- **CAC:** Acquisition costs ÷ new customers acquired, with cost scope and period specified.
- **ROAS:** Attributed revenue ÷ advertising spend; attribution method must be stated.
- **Payback period:** Time required for contribution margin to recover acquisition cost.
- **Activation:** A predefined meaningful first-value event; define it for Reznt.
- **Retention:** Share of a defined customer cohort remaining active over a period.
- **Churn:** Customer or revenue loss over a period, using an explicit definition.
- **Reply time:** Specify first response vs. subsequent response and business-hours treatment.
- **Qualified enquiry rate:** Qualified enquiries ÷ total enquiries, using agreed criteria.
- **Follow-up completion rate:** Follow-ups completed ÷ follow-ups due.
- **Revenue influenced:** Revenue with a defined, documented attribution relationship; not necessarily revenue caused by marketing.

Always report time window, cohort, source, attribution model, sample size where available, and caveats.
