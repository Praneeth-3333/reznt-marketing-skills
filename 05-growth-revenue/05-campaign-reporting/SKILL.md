# Campaign Reporting & Weekly Review Skill

## Purpose
Turn weekly or monthly campaign results into a concise review that drives decisions rather than vanity reporting.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Campaign exports/dashboard values.
- Reporting period and goals.
- Definitions, budget, attribution, and prior-period comparison.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Confirm date range, data source, currency, metric definitions, and attribution.
2. Compare performance to goals and prior comparable periods.
3. Show volume and quality: leads, qualified leads, demos, customers, cost, and downstream conversion where available.
4. Note data-quality issues, tracking changes, and cohort lag.
5. Explain likely drivers only when supported; otherwise state hypotheses.
6. Recommend what to continue, stop, investigate, or test next week.
7. Record owners and due dates.

## Required output
- Executive summary.
- KPI table with goals and period-over-period comparison.
- Wins, misses, causes/evidence, and risks.
- Next-week action list with owners.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not cherry-pick periods or mix attribution models. State when conversion outcomes have not matured yet.
