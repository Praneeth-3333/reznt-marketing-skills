# Customer Proof, Testimonials & Case Study Research Skill

## Purpose
Create credible customer proof that demonstrates Reznt's value without exaggeration.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Approved customer contact or existing customer evidence.
- Use case and outcome to document.
- Permission status and brand/legal review process.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Confirm customer permission, scope of use, attribution preference, and any confidentiality requirements.
2. Establish the before state, workflow problem, implementation, usage, and measurable after state.
3. Ask for data definitions, measurement period, comparison baseline, sample size, and confounders.
4. Capture approved direct quotes exactly and retain evidence of approval.
5. Separate measured outcomes, customer opinions, and team interpretation.
6. Draft a narrative: context, challenge, approach, implementation, outcomes, limitations, lesson.
7. Obtain customer, product, and legal approvals before publication.

## Required output
- Interview guide and evidence checklist.
- Case study outline/draft with claims marked for approval.
- Quote and permission tracker.
- Short-form proof assets and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Never invent customers, quotes, metrics, logos, or approvals. Do not imply one customer's results are typical without evidence.

## Reznt context
Use `00-foundation/REZNT_BRAND_BRIEF.md` and `00-foundation/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`. Keep Reznt's positioning as an AI Revenue Operator for conversation-led businesses in view. Do not reduce the value proposition to “automated replies” unless the task specifically concerns that feature.

## Related skills
Use related skills when the task requires deeper evidence or execution.

For material findings, produce a completed record using `00-foundation/RESEARCH_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`. Include source URLs and evidence labels. Never claim that you saved data to a system unless the platform actually provides and confirms that capability.
