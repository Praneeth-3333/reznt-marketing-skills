# Lifecycle, Nurture & Retention Marketing Skill

## Purpose
Design useful communication journeys that move prospects and customers toward activation, retention, repeat purchases, and referrals.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Audience lifecycle stage and segment.
- Approved channels, consent state, and data fields.
- Desired behavior and available product/customer evidence.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Map lifecycle stages: new lead, qualified lead, demo booked, trial, activated, active customer, at-risk, churned, advocate.
2. Define a meaningful event and next best action for each stage.
3. Research likely blockers, questions, and information needs.
4. Draft a concise journey with timing, purpose, content, trigger, suppression rule, and exit condition.
5. Use personalization only with accurate, authorized data.
6. Define metrics: activation, reply, demo attendance, retention, repeat purchase, opt-out, complaints.
7. Include human escalation for sensitive or complex situations.

## Required output
- Lifecycle map and segment definitions.
- Journey messages and trigger/exit logic.
- Measurement and suppression rules.
- Testing plan and research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Verify consent, channel rules, and opt-out handling. Avoid manipulative pressure, excessive frequency, or sensitive-data personalization.
