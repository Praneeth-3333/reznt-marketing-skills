# Growth Experimentation & CRO Skill

## Purpose
Design prioritized experiments that test Reznt's marketing and conversion hypotheses.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Problem/opportunity and audience.
- Baseline metrics if available.
- Constraints: time, budget, sample, tools, approval.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. State a falsifiable hypothesis and why it might be true.
2. Define audience, intervention, primary metric, guardrail metrics, and observation window.
3. Check whether the test can receive enough traffic/sample to be informative.
4. Change a limited number of variables and avoid overlapping experiments that contaminate results.
5. Estimate impact only with stated assumptions; use directional tests when statistical power is insufficient.
6. Define success, failure, and inconclusive outcomes before launch.
7. Record results and decide whether to ship, iterate, or stop.

## Required output
- Prioritized experiment backlog.
- Experiment brief: hypothesis, audience, variant, metric, guardrails, duration, owner.
- Decision criteria and reporting template.
- Follow-up actions.

## Quality and safety checks
Do not claim statistical significance without an appropriate analysis. Do not optimize a proxy metric at the expense of lead quality or customer trust.
