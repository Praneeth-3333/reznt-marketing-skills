# Marketing Analytics & Performance Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Turn Reznt's campaign and funnel data into clear performance insights and next actions.

## When to invoke
Invoke this skill when a Reznt team member needs the task described above completed independently.

## Required inputs
- Authorized analytics/campaign exports or dashboard metrics.
- Goal, reporting period, definitions, and attribution model.
- Segments/channels to compare.

If a critical input is missing, state the assumption or ask one concise clarification. Do not block useful work when reasonable assumptions can be clearly labeled.

## Operating procedure
1. Confirm metric definitions, date range, timezone, cohort, attribution model, and source.
2. Check data quality, missing fields, duplicate records, tracking changes, and sample sizes.
3. Build a funnel from exposure/visit to lead, qualified lead, demo, activation, and customer where data exists.
4. Compare channels, segments, creatives, and cohorts without mixing incompatible denominators.
5. Identify trends and plausible explanations; label causality as unproven unless supported by design.
6. Quantify uncertainty and avoid overinterpreting small samples.
7. Recommend actions and follow-up analyses.

## Required output
- Executive performance summary.
- KPI table with definitions and periods.
- Funnel/cohort findings and data-quality notes.
- Prioritized actions and experiment ideas.
- Research-log records.

## Quality and safety checks
Never invent metrics or present correlation as causation. Do not expose personal-level data unnecessarily. State when a metric cannot be calculated.
